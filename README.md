CVer is a web application that serves as both a social platform and a personal library for people to expand their horizons in terms of CV creation. 

CVs today are boring and styling them to your hearts content could attract a specific audience if you know where to look.

And the app if you're struggling with the right idea on how to style your CV, there are other users with an open-source mindset who are willing to share their creations with others and to provide both insight and inspiration.

The app features a user-friendly UI, meant to attract potential users with its simplicity.
A CV creator with many gimmicks for users to play around with, allowing them to change the colour, style and add some predefined decorations to make their submittions more appealing!
Users are encouraged to share their creations, however they have the choice to keep it for themselves as well in a specific section under their profile.

A friends list allows users to interact with one another and privately share their creations.

The biggest emphasis this application brings is its simplicity, among other things.


Entities:
1. Users [with varying roles]
2. Templates
3. Styles 
4. Posts
5. Friendships

"Users" is anyone that is using the website. They follow community guidelines and interact with the application as intended. Users are divided into two roles: regular and admin

Templates is the collection of all the different ready-made CVs if users are looking to save themselves the trouble of making a CV style from scratch

Styles is a predefined list of vanity both users and admins have access to, meant to decorate a person's CV or change it's colour.

Posts tracks all the posts made by users or admins for easy tracking in case of guideline breaches

Friendships serves as the connecting web between users, both Admin and regular User
